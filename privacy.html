Privacy Policy

Hear - Volume Limiter

Last Updated:

Introduction

Hear - Volume Limiter ("the App") is a macOS application designed to help protect your hearing by limiting the maximum system volume. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the App.

Data Collection

Hear - Volume Limiter is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or user data. The App operates entirely locally on your device.

Analytics

The App uses Sentry for error tracking and analytics. Sentry is a privacy-focused service that helps us identify and fix technical issues. The information collected by Sentry includes:

  • Anonymous error reports and crash logs
  • Technical information about your device (OS version, app version)
  • No personal identifiers or user-specific data

This data is used solely for improving the App's stability and performance. Sentry's privacy practices comply with GDPR and other privacy regulations.

Local Data Storage

The App stores your preferences (such as volume limits and device selections) locally on your Mac using macOS's standard UserDefaults system. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third parties.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party service:

  • Sentry: For error tracking and analytics (privacy-safe, no personal data collected)

Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete. You can uninstall the App at any time, which will remove all locally stored preferences.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
